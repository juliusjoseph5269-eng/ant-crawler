Ants

Ants are eusocial insects of the family Formicidae, in the order Hymenoptera. They live in colonies that range from a few dozen individuals to millions, with a body divided into a head, a thorax and an abdomen.

Most colonies are led by a queen, with workers that forage and tend the young. Ants communicate through pheromones and follow chemical trails to food. Others, such as leafcutter ants and army ants, farm fungus or hunt in huge columns. Subfamilies include Myrmicinae, Formicinae and the Dorylinae.
